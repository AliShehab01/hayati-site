Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-24

Hayati is a life-simulation game. This policy explains what happens with your information when you play it.

Offline by default. Hayati is fully playable without creating an account. Your life story, wallet and settings are saved on your device. Nothing about how you play is sent anywhere unless you choose to create an optional account (below) or an ad is shown (below).

Optional account. You may choose to create an account to back up your save to the cloud and play across devices. This is handled by Supabase, our backend provider. If you create an account, we store: your email address (or the identifier your sign-in provider gives us for Google/Apple sign-in), and your game save data (your character's life state, wallet balance, and entitlements such as purchased items). We do not ask for your real name, address, or phone number, and the game never uses your real family name for in-game characters.

Android launch list. On our website you can leave your email address to be told when Hayati is released on Google Play. We store only that address and the page language, with Supabase. We use it to send you one email with the store link, and we delete the list after that email is sent. To be removed sooner, write to playhayati@gmail.com.

Ads. Hayati shows ads through Google AdMob to keep the game free. On iOS, the first time the game wants to show a personalized ad, Apple's system will ask for your permission to be tracked across apps ("App Tracking Transparency"). You can say no — the game and its ads work either way; declining only means ads may be less relevant to you, not that you see fewer of them or that anything else in the game changes. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers, general location (such as country), and ad interaction data, under Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Purchases. In-app purchases (removing ads, "Wasta Pass," and coin packs) are handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details — the store providers process payment directly, and we only receive a confirmation that a purchase was made, so we can unlock what you paid for.

Friends, leaderboards and the name other players see. If you sign in you can choose a display name (3 to 16 letters, digits and spaces, no emoji). It is shown to other players on the world and country leaderboards, on your friends' boards and on your public profile, together with your country flag, your level, your best score and whether you own Wasta Pass. You can add friends with a friend code. We store who is friends with whom, and gifts and score duels between friends. To keep invite rewards fair we also keep an anonymous "device hash" (a one-way hash of a random number the app makes on your phone; it is not your advertising id and cannot be turned back into one) so the same phone cannot claim an invite reward for several accounts. Please do not put your real name or personal details in a display name. You can report a name you find offensive (Report name in the menu next to a row) and hide a player on your own phone; reports are read by us and offending names are removed. You can change your display name or delete your account at any time.

Anonymous counters. From version 2.2, when you sign in with Apple or Google you agree to this policy, and from then on the game counts how often things happen, such as lives started, the country chosen or a shop opened, as plain totals per day, app version and phone type. They are not linked to you, your account, your phone or your advertising id, and they never contain your name, your save or anything you typed. They also count how long a session lasts and how many ads and years it held, why an ad was not shown, rewarded-ad offers and purchase steps, and how Buzz and turning points are used (opens, posts, replies, choices), still with no account, device or advertising id, no name, no message text, no money amount and no time finer than the day. If you never sign in, nothing is counted.

Telling us what you think. The first time you start a second life we may ask what you would like to see next. Answering is optional. If you tap Send, we receive the choices you tapped, your words (up to 1,000 characters), the app version, your phone type, the game language and the country of your life, with no name, account or device id. Please do not write personal details in it. To have a message removed, email playhayati@gmail.com and describe it.

Voice packs. Voice clips for calls can be downloaded when you choose to (in Settings). The download is a plain file request to our static file host (hayati-voices.pages.dev); it carries no account or device information beyond what any web request carries (your IP address, which the host sees but we do not store or use).

Sales figures. When Apple tells us about a purchase or a refund, we keep a line with the product, price, country of the store and time, to see how the game is doing. It holds no name, account, device or transaction identifier and cannot be linked to you.

What we don't do. We do not sell your data to anyone. We do not share your data with third parties except the service providers named above (Supabase for optional account storage, Google AdMob for ads, Apple/Google for purchases), each acting under their own privacy terms, and only to the extent needed to provide the service.

Your choices. You can play entirely offline and skip the account feature. You can decline App Tracking Transparency on iOS. You can delete your account and cloud save data at any time by contacting playhayati@gmail.com — we will delete your account row and save data within a reasonable time.

Children. Hayati is rated for a general audience with mild, cartoonish life-simulation themes; it is not directed at young children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described above.

Changes. If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this document. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the update.

Contact. Questions or deletion requests: playhayati@gmail.com
