10 Games Like BitLife in 2026

BitLife turned "living a whole life in your pocket" into a genre. But in 2026 the life-sim world is bigger than one game. Whether you want the closest BitLife alternative, a 3D social world, or a life simulator in Arabic, here are the 10 best games like BitLife you can play right now.

1. Hayati – Life Simulator ⭐ Our Pick

Full disclosure: I'm the developer of Hayati — but I'd rank it #1 even if I weren't, because nothing else on this list does what it does: a free life simulator where you're born in Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, the UAE, Qatar, or Oman and live year by year, with characters speaking each country's actual dialect and phone calls voiced in local accents. The UK and US are playable too, and more countries are coming.

Playable in Arabic and English, with travel, marriage, a prison chapter, school clubs, mini-games, levels, and daily quests. Free on iOS and Android now.

2. InstLife

The closest thing to BitLife itself — a text-based life simulator where you guide a character from birth to death through education, careers, relationships, and random life events. Simple, addictive, and completely free.

3. Alter Ego

A deeper, more introspective take on the life-sim formula: a text-based game about personality, moral choices, and mental health, with multiple endings shaped by who you decide to become.

4. The Sims Mobile

EA's mobile version of the legendary franchise — design your Sim, build a home, and live out careers, friendships, and family life in full 3D.

5. Avakin Life

A 3D social life simulator: create your avatar, decorate your apartment, and hang out with millions of players in a persistent virtual world.

6. Virtual Families 3

Adopt a little person, move them into a (slightly run-down) house, and guide their family through generations — career, kids, and all.

7. Episode: Choose Your Story

Interactive story-based life sim: step into thousands of user-created stories and make the choices that decide love, drama, and destiny.

8. Choices: Stories You Play

Pixelberry's narrative life-sim with high-production stories across romance, mystery, and fantasy — your decisions shape every chapter.

9. IMVU

One of the longest-running avatar life sims: build your 3D identity, chat, dress up, and roleplay entire second lives with a huge community.

10. Second Life

The original virtual life simulator, still going strong — own land, run a business, and live an entirely parallel life online.

FAQ

What is the best game like BitLife in 2026?

It depends what you want. For the closest text-based experience, InstLife. For 3D life simulation, The Sims Mobile. For something genuinely different — a life sim set in the Gulf with Arabic dialects — Hayati is the only option.

Are there any free games like BitLife?

Yes. Hayati, InstLife, and Avakin Life are all free to play, and most games on this list are free with optional in-app purchases.

Is there a life simulator game in Arabic?

Yes — Hayati is fully playable in Arabic and English, with dialogue written in each Gulf country's dialect.

What are the best offline life simulator games?

Text-based sims like InstLife, Alter Ego, and Hayati are great offline-friendly picks, since most of the gameplay doesn't need a constant connection.

Can I play these on iPhone and Android?

All ten games on this list are available (or coming) on both iOS and Android, except Second Life which is primarily desktop.
